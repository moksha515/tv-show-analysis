    Which shows get renewed?

    We predict renewal from IMDb rating, vote count, network, genre, and season 1 episode count. The model returns a renewal probability for every show.

    Do shows get worse over time?

    Only shows with more than one season, and mostly those that kept getting renewed. We compare season 1 to the final season and measure how often ratings drop.
